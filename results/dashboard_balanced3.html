🕵️ Primary emotion — LLM vs. word list

The LLM predicts one of 8 emotions per review (same call as sentiment). The NRC word list scores each review's words and takes the highest emotion; ties are broken alphabetically (anger, anticipation, disgust, fear…) — which is why anticipation is common in the word-list column.
